### MapLibre and OpenStreetMap

RiverCall uses MapLibre technology to display maps based on OpenStreetMap data.

MapLibre is a mapping library; map tiles and related resources may be supplied by separate providers. When these resources are requested from external servers, those servers may receive technical information such as your IP address and request metadata.

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). See [OpenStreetMap Copyright and Licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The applicable privacy practices depend on the map tile provider configured in RiverCall.
