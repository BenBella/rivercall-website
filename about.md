# About RiverCall

RiverCall is a mobile application developed and maintained by **Lukáš Andrlík**. Designed for kayakers, canoeists, rafters, and outdoor enthusiasts, RiverCall provides real-time river data, whitewater section details, and water gauge measurements across European rivers.

## What RiverCall Offers

- **Interactive Map & Exploration**: Explore river sections and gauge stations on an interactive map powered by MapLibre, OpenFreeMap, and OpenStreetMap.
- **Whitewater Section Guides**: Access section difficulty grades, length, vertical drop, put-in/take-out coordinates, and river descriptions.
- **Gauge Readings & Trend Charts**: View current and historical water level and flow rate measurements with intuitive trend charts.
- **Water Level Calibrations**: Easily see if a section is runnable based on Low Water (LW), Medium Water (MW), and High Water (HW) calibration thresholds.
- **Favorites & Search**: Save your favorite rivers and gauge stations for quick access, or search across rivers, sections, and stations.
