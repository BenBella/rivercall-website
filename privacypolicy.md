# RiverCall Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

## 1. About RiverCall

RiverCall is a freemium mobile application developed and maintained by Lukáš Andrlík (“the developer”). It provides river information and water conditions to help users plan outdoor activities and whitewater kayaking trips.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the RiverCall application.

## 2. Partners & Sources

### 2.1 River Data Source — Rivermap API

RiverCall retrieves public river information and water measurements from the Rivermap API.

The RiverCall backend provides public river data and does not maintain user accounts or user profiles as part of the functionality described in this policy.

When the application communicates with the API, technical information such as an IP address may be processed by the API server or its hosting infrastructure. The information actually recorded depends on the API and hosting configuration.

### 2.2 Map Data — MapLibre and OpenStreetMap

RiverCall uses MapLibre technology to display maps based on OpenStreetMap data.

MapLibre is a mapping library; map tiles and related resources may be supplied by separate providers. When these resources are requested from external servers, those servers may receive technical information such as your IP address and request metadata.

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). See [OpenStreetMap Copyright and Licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The applicable privacy practices depend on the map tile provider configured in RiverCall.

## 3. Information Processed by RiverCall

### 3.1 Location Information

RiverCall may request access to your device's location to provide location-related features.

Location is used locally on your device and is not transmitted to the RiverCall backend by the location functionality described in this policy.

You can manage or revoke location permission in your device settings. Disabling permission may affect location-related features.

### 3.2 Data Stored on Your Device

RiverCall may store application settings, preferences, favorites, and other application data locally on your device, depending on the features you use.

Data stored locally is not automatically transmitted to the RiverCall backend unless a particular feature explicitly requires it.

### 3.3 Technical Information

When RiverCall communicates with online services, technical information may be processed as part of the network connection. This can include IP addresses and request metadata, depending on the service and its configuration.

The RiverCall backend provides public river information and does not maintain user accounts or user profiles as part of the functionality described in this policy.

## 4. Analytics & Diagnostics

### 4.1 Firebase Crashlytics

RiverCall uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to identify and investigate application crashes and improve application stability.

Depending on the platform and SDK configuration, Crashlytics may process crash reports, stack traces, device and operating system information, application version, timestamps, and technical installation identifiers.

Diagnostic information may be transmitted to Google for processing. For more information, see [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### 4.2 Google Analytics

RiverCall uses Google Analytics to understand application usage and improve its functionality and user experience.

Depending on the SDK configuration, Google Analytics may collect information such as application interactions, events, session information, device and operating system information, and technical identifiers. Other data categories may be collected depending on the implementation and configuration.

This information may be transmitted to Google for analytics and reporting purposes. For more information, see [Google Analytics privacy information](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through RiverCall is used, as applicable, to:

- Provide river information, measurements, and application features.
- Display maps and related resources.
- Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
- Understand application usage and improve the user experience.
- Maintain the security and reliability of the application.
- Respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

The information collected depends on the features used and the configuration of the relevant services.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

RiverCall relies on the following services:

- **Rivermap API:** Public river information and water measurements.
- **MapLibre:** Map-rendering technology. Map tiles and related resources may be provided by separate providers.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** Crash reporting and diagnostics.
- **Google Analytics:** Application usage analytics.

These services may process technical information as necessary to provide their functionality. Their roles, data processing practices, and retention periods depend on the service and applicable configuration.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

RiverCall does not maintain user accounts or user profiles as part of the backend functionality described in this policy.

Data stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. This does not necessarily delete information already transmitted to third-party services or retained in server logs.

Diagnostic and analytics data are retained according to the relevant service settings and provider retention practices.

For details about Google's data practices, consult the [Firebase Privacy and Security documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed through RiverCall. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. International Data Processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, including countries outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, personal data transfers are subject to the safeguards required by relevant data protection law.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise applicable rights or ask questions about this policy, contact the developer using the details below.

## 11. Children's Privacy

RiverCall is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe personal data has been collected improperly, please contact the developer so that the matter can be investigated and appropriate action taken.

## 12. Website Cookies

The RiverCall website may use cookies or similar technologies depending on its configuration and the services integrated into it.

The use of analytics, cookies, or other tracking technologies on the website must be assessed separately from the mobile application. Where required, appropriate information and consent mechanisms will be provided.

## 13. Links to Other Websites

RiverCall may contain links to third-party websites. These websites operate under their own privacy policies and practices. The developer is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.
